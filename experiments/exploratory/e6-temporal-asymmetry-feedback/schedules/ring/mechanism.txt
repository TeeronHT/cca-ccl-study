Workload::issue_coll_comm starts that rank's Ring when its own COMP node finishes.
There is no collective-wide barrier in the workload layer.

Ring::ready posts the step's send and its recv in the same call. With
--rendezvous-protocol, the bulk bytes wait until the peer has posted the
matching recv. A hop that touches rank 15 therefore does not inject its
payload before rank 15's COMP delay ends. Hops among ranks that are already
ready can proceed.

With TRANSPORT_CONNECTION_MODE PERSISTENT every message from rank i to rank
i+1 (payload) and from i+1 to i (the 8 KiB rendezvous handshake) is posted on
that directed pair's single queue pair, so DCQCN/HPCC state and timers carry
from one hop to the next. Queue occupancy carries as before. The Ring has no
precondition/completion phases, so the state-overlap metric is defined only
for the StragglAR schedule.
